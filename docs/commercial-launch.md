Status: **superseded commercial-license plan; MIT Developer Preview selected**.

WebKitUI MCP 0.6.1 Developer Preview is intended to launch free of charge under
the MIT License. No checkout, activation key, evaluation window, seat limit or
commercial-use restriction is part of this release.

The launch objective is adoption and technical feedback through the public
repository, a notarized direct-download artifact and developer communities.
Publication, signing, notarization, installation and promotional posts remain
separate approval gates.

Future paid support, hosting or managed services may be offered separately.
They must not narrow or revoke the MIT rights granted for versions already
distributed under MIT.
