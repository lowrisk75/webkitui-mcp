## Useful engineering findings

- Cardinality must describe candidates remaining after the complete locator
  recipe, not raw matches from one broad selector.
- A virtualized row can retain role, label, pointer, and geometry while its
  record context changes; deterministic neighborhood facts are therefore part
  of the semantic fingerprint.
- Missing semantic, identity, or geometry evidence must remain `unknown` and
  must not be forced into a failure counter.
- Overlay interception and frame/world access are distinct future causes that
  the pure core cannot observe yet.

## Rejected or conjectural

- Notebook-generated code classified every semantic mismatch as proven logical
  target change and initially treated `unknown` as a design error. Both claims
  were rejected: the core preserves `unknown` to avoid fabricated certainty.
- The suggested topology hash used Swift `hashValue`, which is randomized
  between processes and therefore unsuitable for durable telemetry. The core
  uses explicit semantic facts instead.
- Claims of sub-millisecond fingerprinting were not accompanied by a local
  measurement and are not adopted.
